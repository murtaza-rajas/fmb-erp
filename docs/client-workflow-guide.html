FMB Nagpur · Procurement & Payment System

From "we're low on stock" to "the vendor's been paid" — one connected system

This is a plain-language walkthrough of how a purchase moves through the system end to end, who is responsible at each point, and how the different parts of the software connect to form one continuous, auditable chain — from a store raising a request, to the vendor receiving payment.

01

The seven-step flow

Every purchase — routine or urgent — follows the same path.

No step can be skipped, and no payment can happen without the three documents behind it — the Purchase Order, the Goods Receipt, and the Vendor Invoice — all agreeing with each other.

STEP 1

Stock check & requisition

A store notices stock is running low and raises a Purchase Requisition — item, quantity, needed-by date, reason.

Store
STEP 2

PO created & sent

The Procurement Head creates the Purchase Order and sends it to the vendor directly — no separate sign-off step.

Procurement Head
STEP 3

Goods received

The store logs a Goods Receipt Note recording what actually arrived against the order.

Store
STEP 4

Quality & quantity check

Shortages keep the order open for the balance. Damaged or rejected goods are logged against the vendor and excluded from payment.

Store
STEP 5

Invoice matched

The vendor's invoice is checked against the order and the receipt — quantity and rate must agree across all three.

Purchase
STEP 6

Verified & approved

The matched set is reviewed against the vendor's ledger — advances, TDS, credit terms — and the payment voucher is approved.

Finance & HR
STEP 7

Paid & closed

Payment is transferred, an advice is sent to the vendor, and the order is marked Closed.

03

Who does what

Four teams, each responsible for a distinct part of the chain.

Store

Monitors stock, raises requisitions, receives goods, creates the Goods Receipt Note, and checks quality & quantity.

Procurement Head

Creates the Purchase Order and sends it to the vendor.

Purchase

Matches the vendor's invoice against the Purchase Order and Goods Receipt.

Finance & HR

Verifies and approves payment, processes it, and maintains the vendor ledger.

One rule that's enforced, not just written down: payment approval is restricted to paid-staff roles — never to Khidmat Gujar (volunteer) staff — consistent with FMB's existing staff structure. This is checked in three separate places in the system, so it can't be granted by accident through a role change.

Tracking a purchase order

Every order carries a visible status, so its progress is never a mystery.

A Purchase Order moves through eight states in order. Anyone looking at it can see exactly where it stands — nothing moves forward silently.

Draft→ Issued→ Partially Received→ Received→ Invoiced→ Payment Pending→ Paid→ Closed
05

Built-in safeguards

Rules that hold regardless of who's using the system.

1

Orders go out fast, on purpose

The Procurement Head issues a Purchase Order directly — there's deliberately no separate sign-off step slowing that down.

2

One approval gate for every payment

Finance & HR is the single point where a payment gets approved — no payment reaches a vendor without passing through it.

3

Stock reflects reality, not paperwork timing

Stock counts update the moment goods physically arrive — not whenever the vendor eventually gets paid.

4

No match, no payment

A payment simply cannot be raised unless its Purchase Order, Goods Receipt, and Vendor Invoice all agree — this is structural, not a manual checklist someone could skip.

06

When things don't go to plan

The system has a defined path for every common exception.

SituationWhat happens
Short supply on deliveryA partial Goods Receipt is created; the order stays open for the remaining balance.
Damaged or rejected goodsLogged as a debit note against the vendor and excluded from payment.
Invoice doesn't match the order or receiptHeld at the three-way match stage until it's resolved with the vendor.
Urgent or emergency purchaseA requisition and order are still required within 24 hours, retrospectively — and it still goes through the approval matrix.
Stock returnHandled as its own process, issuing a debit note and adjusting the stock ledger accordingly.
